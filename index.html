MVSIQVA

Your new favorite musical website

01

Who are we ?

We are 2 students passionated about music. MVSIQVA is our end-year project - a musical website thinked to discover, listening to music and be informed about the musical news.

02

What we do

MVSIQVA offers an integrated music player, a ranking of current tracks, the latest news from the music industry and a customizable profile space, all in a dark and elegant interface.

03

Technologies

HTML5, CSS3 et JavaScript vanilla, without framework. Data is managed via localStorage and calls to public APIs. The project is hosted on GitHub Pages and versioned on GitHub.
